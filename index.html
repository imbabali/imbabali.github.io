Buyonjo: Mobile-Money Bill Payments

Beta

A bridge between waste-collection customers and the companies that serve them. A customer picks their company, types a client ID or phone number, sees what they owe and approves a mobile-money prompt with their PIN, with no account to create. Companies subscribe to be listed and connect their own MTN or Airtel merchant account, so the money settles straight to them and Buyonjo never holds customer funds. The Android and iOS apps wrap the same web app. Built with Claude Code, with payments in operator sandbox testing ahead of launch.

Health Data Work

The engines behind the numbers.

Three recent examples of the data systems I build in public health practice. They are anonymised: the client data stays with the client, and what follows describes the method.

Independent verification · PwC Uganda · 2026

Verifying hospital performance before a donor pays for it

Donor-funded grant payments to 17 regional referral hospitals are released against each hospital's reported performance. The question was whether each hospital's own records could prove what it had reported, so that payment rested on verified results rather than self-reported figures.

What I built

I led the strategic information work and built the engine the verification ran on: a 492-question Qualtrics instrument, reported-versus-verified indicator tables, a dashboard and charts. Together they gave one consistent, auditable basis for judging 26 payment-linked indicators across every hospital.

  1. Desk review of national-system data in DHIS2 and the EMR
  2. Five-day on-site checks of registers and patient files at each hospital
  3. 492-question verification instrument in Qualtrics
  4. Reported-versus-verified tables for 26 payment-linked indicators
  5. Dashboard, charts and the quarterly verification report

The verification ran again over a second quarter with a new indicator, adding hospital-level integrity audits that caught data errors before results reached the client. I co-authored the quarterly report, which the client adopted as the deliverable of record.

Fiduciary risk assessment · PwC Uganda · 2026

Testing whether government health entities are ready for direct funding

A move to government-to-government financing depends on whether ministries, medical stores, hospitals and district governments can receive funds on-budget and prove the milestones that release payment. As Monitoring and Evaluation Expert on the assessment, I owned daily data quality assurance across the national field deployment.

What I built

Assessment tools tailored to each kind of entity, informed by recurring weaknesses mined from prior audits, and a test of whether each entity's data and reporting systems could track and prove payment milestones.

  1. Prior audits mined for recurring weaknesses
  2. Tools tailored to the ministry, national medical stores, hospitals and district governments
  3. Every field team's evidence checked against source documents in real time
  4. Data systems tested for milestone tracking: DHIS2, iHRIS, HCMS, eLMIS
  5. Risks and strengths rated on the national risk matrix

The findings went to a Kampala stakeholder workshop, where the funder and the government agreed the mitigation priorities to fix before funds flow.

Baseline assessment · Uganda · 2026

A reproducible baseline pipeline for HIV prevention among adolescent girls and young women

A national HIV-prevention plan for adolescent girls and young women needed a baseline in two districts. Each district was to be described on its own, with health facilities shown by level and schools by primary, secondary and tertiary.

What I built

A scripted pipeline that turns the raw survey exports into one report per district and a dashboard with a tab per district. Every figure is computed from the raw exports and shows its base n. Facility level is parsed from the site name, and the readiness scorecard is labelled as a construct: the mean of named, computed indicators.

  1. Four ODK and KoboToolbox exports (community, district, health facility, school) and 21 focus-group and key-informant transcripts
  2. Tabulation engine with validation, run on the untouched raw files
  3. Data layer that feeds the charts, reports and dashboard
  4. Infographics: readiness scorecard, by-level heatmaps, service cascade
  5. Two district reports and a district-tab dashboard

The dashboard carries a live-analysis sheet that recomputes its figures from the data, and that check stands at zero mismatches.
